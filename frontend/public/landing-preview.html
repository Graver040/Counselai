FAQ

Questions, answered

How is this different from ChatGPT?

CounselAI only answers from the documents you upload and cites the exact page for every claim. A general chatbot draws on training data and can invent sections, figures or case law — a real risk in a formal reply.

What kinds of notices does it handle?

It's built for Indian tax and legal notices — CBDT income-tax and GST notices in particular — and can also draft legal letters and client memos. Because it works from your documents, it adapts to whatever you upload.

Can it read scanned or photographed notices?

Yes. Scanned pages are processed with OCR automatically, so you don't have to retype anything. PDFs up to 25 MB are supported.

Does it work in Hindi?

Yes — CounselAI supports both Hindi and English documents and questions.

Is my data private?

Documents are isolated to your own workspace and are never used to train foundation models. You can delete a document — and its underlying vectors — at any time.

What counts as an "AI action"?

Each Ask, Draft or Checklist request counts as one action against your daily limit. Uploading and reading documents does not.
